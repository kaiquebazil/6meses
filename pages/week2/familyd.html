📖 Learning Summary

🎯 Key Vocabulary from PDF

Husband & Wife: Married couple

Parents: Father and mother (not "parentes")

Siblings: Brothers and sisters together

Stepfamily: Stepmother, stepfather, stepsiblings

Grand: Grandfather, grandmother, granddaughter

Nephew/Niece: Son/daughter of your sibling

Cousin: Child of your aunt/uncle

💡 Learning Tips from PDF

Repetition: "Fluency comes from dozens of contextualized repetitions."

No Translation: "Calma que você vai deduzir tudo. Sem precisar traduzir."

Real Life: "Never focusing on the language, but focusing on real life."

Saturation: "Learning ≠ Knowing ≠ Mastering ≠ Speaking. Speaking only comes with saturation."

Perspective: Practice by saying "If I am Isabella..." to change viewpoints.
